Our Story

Crafted in Grasse,
Worn Everywhere

Perfuom began in a small atelier in Grasse, France, where our founding perfumer spent a decade apprenticing under the region's oldest fragrance houses. Every bottle we release is composed by hand, aged in glass for a minimum of six weeks, and tested for a wear time of twelve hours or more.

We work with a small circle of growers for our absolutes — Bulgarian rose, Mysore sandalwood, Calabrian bergamot — so what you smell is the material itself, not an approximation of it.
